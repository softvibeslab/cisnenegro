## Prompt final

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: Square digital menu illustration for the Mexican brewery Cisne Negro, matching its existing illustrated beer menu series.
Primary request: Create a polished new illustration for the draught beer COLCHITA. This is editorial menu art, not an official bottle or can label.
Input images:
1. Henry IX illustration: style reference ONLY for the hand-inked glass, logo treatment, warm cream paper, loose lettering and simple graphic language. Do NOT copy the beer name, IPA wording, alcohol level, crown or hops.
2. Official brewery photo of chamorro and two beers: factual product reference ONLY for the RIGHT-HAND glass marked in blue as Colchita Kölsch 4.5% ABV. Depict that golden beer, white foam, tall straight-sided gently tapered glass without handle, and black CISNE lightning-bolt NEGRO wordmark. Do NOT include the left mug, blue and white diamond crest, food, plate or photo background.
Scene/backdrop: warm cream paper, very subtle print grain, lots of breathable space.
Subject: one large attractive illustrated glass of golden Colchita beer with dense off-white foam, tiny bubbles and a simple warm shadow. Keep the black Cisne Negro brand lettering on the glass crisp and legible.
Style/medium: hand-drawn black ink editorial poster, confident irregular outlines, flat golden color with subtle grain and minimal highlights; stylish independent craft brewery illustration, consistent with reference 1. No photography, no 3D.
Composition/framing: square 1:1; bold large hand-lettered COLCHITA across top; glass dominates center-left, fully inside frame; on the right a small turquoise handwritten annotation with curved arrow pointing toward the glass reads KÖLSCH and 4.5% ABV on separate lines. Cream margin around all edges. Balance the spacious right-hand typography against the glass. No duplicate tiny labels.
Color palette: cream #FBF6E6, near-black #16120E, straw-gold beer, muted turquoise annotation inspired by the actual official photo.
Text (verbatim): "COLCHITA", "KÖLSCH", "4.5% ABV", "CISNE ⚡ NEGRO". On the glass the bolt is a simple black lightning mark between the words. Only these texts.
Constraints: Spell C-O-L-C-H-I-T-A exactly; umlaut on Ö in KÖLSCH; 4.5% ABV exactly. Brand name readable. No invented tasting notes, IBU, awards, origin claims, slogan or official-label claim.
Avoid: cans, bottles, fake packaging label, blankets, mascots, crown, hops, fruit, food, human characters, blue-white Bavarian diamonds, decorative clutter, watermark, cropped glass, text outside the image.
Output one finished square illustration at 1024x1024 or higher.
```
